# Public datasets

## Discovery: GSE308859
Longitudinal mouse transverse aortic constriction (TAC) study integrating single-cell RNA-seq and Visium spatial transcriptomics across Sham, 2-week, 4-week, and 6-week post-TAC conditions.

Final analysis atlas:
- scRNA-seq: 25,186 cells; 32,285 genes; 29 clusters; 15 broad and 23 refined identities.
- Spatial transcriptomics: 7,079 spots.

The public deposition contains one scRNA-seq library and one spatial library per condition. The deposition does not establish whether each transcriptomic library represents one animal or pooled material. Discovery comparisons are therefore descriptive; cells and spots are not treated as biological replicates.

## Validation 1: GSE155882
Source study: Alexanian et al., Nature (2021), "A transcriptional switch governs fibroblast activation in heart disease."

For this project, untreated Sham and TAC scRNA-seq libraries were used:
- 2 Sham biological libraries
- 2 TAC biological libraries
- 2-week TAC
- final atlas: 28,388 singlets

## Validation 2: GSE270896
Source study: Gao et al., Circulation Research (2024), "Glucagon Receptor Antagonist for Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction."

For this project, only the lean C57BL/6J cohort was used:
- 2 Sham biological libraries
- 2 TAC biological libraries
- 10 weeks after TAC/Sham
- whole-heart 10x 3' snRNA-seq
- final atlas: 15,747 nuclei/cells

## Data policy
Raw GEO files are not duplicated in this repository. They should be downloaded directly from NCBI GEO using the accession numbers above.
